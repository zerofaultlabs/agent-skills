# Spring Hills church context

## Mission

Official mission: “Come to Jesus, grow in faith, live in community and make a difference.”

Use the mission as a directional test, not a slogan that must appear in every caption. Strong social content should help someone encounter Jesus, take a step in faith, experience belonging, or serve others.

Source: https://www.springhills.org/mission

## Audience

Spring Hills serves people throughout Sonoma County at every stage of their spiritual journey. Weekend services are especially designed for people who do not yet have a genuine relationship with God through Jesus. The church is multigenerational and serves children, students, adults, seniors, families, and people with disabilities.

Write so a newcomer can understand the post. Do not assume extensive church vocabulary or prior Bible knowledge.

## Belief guardrails

- Treat the Bible as the final authority.
- Present one God in Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
- Present Jesus as fully God and man, crucified for sin, bodily risen, and returning.
- Present salvation as received through trusting Christ, not earned through human merit.
- Present Christian growth as involving Scripture, Christian community, and the Holy Spirit.
- Make Christ known in culturally understandable language without compromising biblical teaching.

For sensitive doctrine, controversy, exact quotations, or claims beyond the supplied sources, request review rather than improvising.

Source: https://www.springhills.org/doctrine

## Belonging and care

Recurring institutional language emphasizes:

- A loving community called family
- Relationships marked by love, support, and belonging
- Meeting people where they are
- Life being better together
- Practical care, prayer, healing, and Christian support
- A place for people of different ages, abilities, and life situations
