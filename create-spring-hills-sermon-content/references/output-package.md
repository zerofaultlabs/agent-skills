# Output package

Default to the two-stage structure below. Omit sections the user did not request when a narrower answer is more useful.

## Stage 1: Sermon Direction Brief

Use this stage before writing finished deliverables when editorial input is useful.

Maintain two layers:

1. **Internal direction brief:** keep the full evidence and tradeoffs below in the saved artifact and tracker.
2. **Nastia Quick view:** send a concise Slack summary containing the sermon link, `My recommendation: A/B/C — short name` near the top, and A, B, and C with a proposed Tuesday cover/title plus the Thursday reel's exact timestamp range and duration. End with exactly one request: `Reply A, B, C, or use your recommendation.`

Do not copy the complete internal direction brief into Slack. Put optional explanation in the same message's thread only when it helps answer a question.

### Sermon snapshot

- **Central truth:** one source-grounded sentence.
- **Audience need:** the ordinary-life tension or opportunity.
- **Source anchor:** the verified passage or controlling sermon section.

### Three paired weekly directions

Give exactly three genuinely different directions labeled A, B, and C. For each provide:

- a short direction name and one-sentence concept;
- **Tuesday preview:** the recommended format, a concrete working cover or first-slide title, and what the post would teach;
- **Thursday preview:** the reel's verified start timestamp, end timestamp, calculated duration, and what the clip would make people feel, understand, or do;
- **Audience value:** why the pairing is useful now;
- **Strength:** why this source material fits the direction;
- **Caution:** the editorial tradeoff or risk.

The two posts must have complementary jobs. Do not present three cosmetic hook variations as three directions.

Every reel preview must come from a verified, continuous transcript passage. Check that its opening is understandable, its ending completes the thought, and its duration fits the 60–90 second target. If no valid timed clip supports a direction, label the timing unavailable or adjust the direction; never invent a range.

### Recommendation and choice

Recommend one direction with a plain-language reason. State why the strongest rejected direction lost. Ask the reviewer to reply with **A**, **B**, **C**, or **use your recommendation**, plus any adjustment. Then stop.

## Stage 2: Production Package

After a direction is chosen, briefly restate the selected direction and produce the package below. Do not reopen settled strategy unless the transcript creates a factual or theological problem.

For Nastia, lead with **What to use** and list the Tuesday deliverable, Thursday timestamps, captions or artifact links, and any required verification. Keep analysis and editing rationale below the ready-to-use material or in an attachment.

### 1. Editorial recommendation

- **Central takeaway:** one source-grounded sentence.
- **Audience connection:** the ordinary-life tension or need.
- **Recommended split:** what Tuesday teaches and what Thursday applies.
- **Do not lead with:** the strongest rejected candidate and why it lost.

### 2. Tuesday carousel or graphic

Provide:

- format recommendation;
- cover or graphic headline;
- four to six carousel slides when a carousel is selected;
- concise design-direction notes only when they affect comprehension;
- ready-to-use caption in the Spring Hills voice;
- exact Scripture reference only when verified.

Make each carousel slide advance the idea. Do not turn sermon outline headings into slides without rewriting them for a social audience.

### 3. Thursday reel

Provide:

- exact start and end timestamps;
- calculated duration;
- first and final transcript words so the editor can locate the cut;
- a one-sentence reason the passage works;
- on-screen hook written as social copy, clearly separate from transcript wording;
- ready-to-use caption in the Spring Hills voice;
- one trimming note if the selected boundary contains a removable pause or verbal stumble.

If no valid 60–90 second continuous clip exists, say so. Offer the best shorter/longer passage or a clearly labeled edit concept rather than fabricating a compliant cut.

### 4. Optional alternative

Include at most two alternatives only when they represent genuinely different central ideas or formats. For each, state the tradeoff in one sentence.

### 5. Verification notes

List only unresolved facts that matter before publishing, such as uncertain transcript wording, an unverified Scripture reference, or missing event logistics. Do not ask for feedback when the evidence already resolves the choice.
