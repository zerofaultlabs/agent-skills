# Selection Rubric

Score each candidate from 0–2 on the seven dimensions below. Use scores to discipline judgment, not to disguise weak evidence.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mission fit | No clear connection | Indirect connection | Clearly supports Jesus, faith, community, or difference-making |
| Audience value | Mostly about the account | Some reader value | Gives a newcomer, member, or local audience a clear reason to care |
| Freshness evidence | Undated, inaccessible, stale, or already logged | One recent idea signal or inspiration reference | Verified repeated pattern or trend under the skill's evidence thresholds |
| Feasibility | Requires unavailable people, access, or production | Possible with moderate coordination | Uses likely weekly assets and low extra effort |
| Distinctiveness | Duplicates the existing week/feed | Familiar but useful | Adds a clear new job, angle, or format |
| Voice and theology | Forced, risky, or unsupported | Adaptable with caution | Naturally warm, accurate, hopeful, and Christ-centered |
| Source integrity | Copies execution or lacks a direct source | Mechanic is separable | Clearly attributes evidence and transforms the mechanic |

## Ranking rules

- Recommend 11–14 point ideas first.
- Keep 8–10 point ideas only when they fill an important content need and name the caution.
- Reject ideas below 8.
- A zero in voice and theology or source integrity is disqualifying regardless of total.
- Do not let freshness compensate for poor fit or high production burden.
- Prefer a balanced final set: do not return three near-identical reels or three generic encouragement graphics.
- Returning zero ideas is correct when no candidate reaches 8 points, a disqualifying zero is present, or the visible ideas add nothing materially new.

## Confidence labels

- **High:** strong fit, feasible, and supported by a verified trend or repeated pattern.
- **Medium:** strong fit and feasible, but supported by one current source or missing a minor execution fact.
- **Exploratory:** worth testing, but format meaning, assets, or Spring Hills fit needs editorial confirmation.
