## Spring Hills filters

- Mission: help people come to Jesus, grow in faith, live in community, and make a difference.
- Audience: people throughout Sonoma County at every stage of their spiritual journey, with a strong newcomer and seeker welcome.
- Voice: warm, upbeat, Bible-grounded, practical, hopeful, Christ-centered, and community-oriented.
- Content mix: sermon reels and teaching, Scripture or sermon carousels, weekend recaps, invitations, community moments, testimonies, and seasonal posts.
- Cadence: no Monday post; Tuesday sermon/graphic; Wednesday development; Thursday sermon reel; Friday invitation; Saturday and Sunday live/community coverage.
- Evidence: use confirmed likes directionally only. Unknown engagement remains unknown and is not a reason to add work for Nastia.

Use the Spring Hills Tracker as the audit trail for later source additions, likes/dislikes, and confirmed editorial rules.
